About the Platform

Turning routine marks and attendance into timely academic support.

{{ metrics.institution_name|default:portal_institution_name }} runs EduPredict on a pure Django and MongoDB Atlas architecture to connect classroom records, predictive machine learning, and student outreach without manual spreadsheet delays.

01 / INGESTION

3-Column Data Pipeline

Teachers upload student ID, marks, and attendance sheets via CSV or Excel. Missing values and outliers are flagged before modeling.

02 / PREDICTION

Ensemble Risk Scoring

Ridge Regression, Random Forest, and Isolation Forest models evaluate performance trends and classify Low, Medium, or High risk.

03 / INTERVENTION

Early Alerts & Transcripts

High-risk students receive actionable alerts and study guidance while faculty and admins track resolution through support tickets.
