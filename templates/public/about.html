About EduPredict

One clear academic workflow from classroom marks to early intervention.

EduPredict is built with Python Django, MongoDB Atlas, and Scikit-Learn to replace fragmented spreadsheets with a unified, role-isolated early warning system for schools, colleges, and universities.

4-Role Architecture

How data moves cleanly through EduPredict

01 / ADMIN

Structure & Governance

Creates courses, links registered Teachers, approves faculty accounts, sets risk thresholds, and reviews support tickets and audit logs.

02 / TEACHER

Marks & Interventions

Uploads 3-column CSV/Excel sheets or logs quick marks & attendance for assigned classes, triggering automatic ML scoring and student alerts.

03 / ANALYST

Data & ML Pipeline

Cleans batch datasets, runs RandomForest, Ridge Regression, and IsolationForest pipelines, and audits accuracy & course demand.

04 / STUDENT

Clarity & Growth

Views Program/Class, Assigned Teachers, predicted GPA, study tips, teacher alerts, and formal A4 printable PDF transcripts.
