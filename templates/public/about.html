4-Role Campus Architecture

How every role works together cleanly

01 / ADMIN

Courses, Users & Approvals

Configures courses and Program/Batch levels, approves registered Teachers and Data Analysts, manages users, and resolves support tickets.

02 / TEACHER

Multi-Format Marks Upload

Registers for Admin approval, uploads marks and attendance in .csv, .xlsx, .json, .jsonl, .tsv, or .txt, and logs quick entries with auto-alerts.

03 / ANALYST

ML Pipeline & Accuracy

Registers for Admin approval, cleans batch datasets, and runs Ridge, RandomForest, and IsolationForest pipelines to score GPA and anomalies.

04 / STUDENT

Forecasts & A4 Transcripts

Provisioned automatically by teacher uploads. Tracks GPA forecasts, course marks, study tips, and prints formal monochrome A4 PDF transcripts.
