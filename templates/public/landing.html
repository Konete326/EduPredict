Four Dedicated Panels

One platform, clear role boundaries

Each account signs into a focused workspace with strict permissions and role-appropriate tools.

Admin

Approves faculty accounts, manages courses and portal branding, reviews support tickets, and monitors system health.

Teacher

Uploads 3-column marks and attendance sheets, tracks class averages, and sends early alerts to at-risk students.

Student

Views personal attendance, subject marks, risk forecast, study tips, and downloads official A4 academic transcripts.

Data Analyst

Cleans uploaded datasets, trains regression and anomaly models, compares model accuracy, and inspects risk clusters.
